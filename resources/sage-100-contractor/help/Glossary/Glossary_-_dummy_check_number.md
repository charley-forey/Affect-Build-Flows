You are here: [Glossary](Glossary.md) > dummy cheque number

A false cheque number. Most payroll records assigned pay type **3-Hand Computed**, **4-Startup**, or **6-Third Party** result in a net $0 pay cheque. Records using those pay types do not require you to print a cheque, but do require a chequenumber.
