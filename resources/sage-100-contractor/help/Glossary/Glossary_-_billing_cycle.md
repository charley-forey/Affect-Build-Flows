You are here: [Glossary](Glossary.md) > billing cycle

The period of time between statement dates. In Sage 100 Contractor, a cycle is represented by ##DY (a number of days), ##MO (a number of months), and ##TH (a specified day every month).

- **##DY** – 30DY means due 30 days from the date of the invoice.
- **##MO** – 02MO means due two months from the date of the invoice.
- **##TH** – 25TH means due on the 25th day of each month regardless of the date of the invoice.
