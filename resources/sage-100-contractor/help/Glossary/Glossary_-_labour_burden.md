You are here: [Glossary](Glossary.md) > labour burden

Labour burden refers to job-related payroll overhead costs. These employer-incurred costs are in addition to the direct wages and include taxes, insurance, and benefits. The additional costs for Labour burden can account for up to 35% of the total wages.

For more detailed information, see the topic [Working with Project Management](../Modules/6-Project_Management/Working_with_Project_Management.md) and related topics in that section of the Help.
