### Printing screen captures of Lookup windows

Although many Lookup windows have a **Print** button, some do not. For example, in the **1-5 Bank Reconciliation** window, when you click the drop-down for the **Account#** box, the **Ledger Accounts** Lookup window that appears has no **Print** button. However, you can capture information from a Lookup window to your clipboard, paste it into a Word document, and then print the document.

#### To capture and print a Lookup window:

1. Open the Lookup window, and then click the **Maximize** button (center button, upper right of the window).
2. To reduce the amount of information displayed, select a portion of the data. For example, instead of displaying **All Account Types**, select one account type, perhaps **Cash Accounts**.
3. Press Ctrl+Print Screen to capture the information to your clipboard.
4. Start Microsoft Word, open a blank document, and then press Ctrl+V to paste the screen capture into the Word document.
5. Print the Word document.
