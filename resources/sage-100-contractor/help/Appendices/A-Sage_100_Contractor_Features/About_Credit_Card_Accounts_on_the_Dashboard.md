### About Credit Card Accounts on the Dashboard

Displays five selected credit card accounts with their balances.

Displays the first five credit card accounts in your list of credit card accounts. If these five accounts are not the ones you are interested in monitoring, you can click the **Edit** button to the right of the title heading and select five accounts from your credit card accounts list.

Tip: The bottom line in the list of accounts, **All Other Card Accounts**, presents the sum of all other existing cash accounts in that company.
