### Converting measurements on the calculator

#### To convert measurements on the calculator:

|  | 1 | Open the calculator. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Enter the number you want to convert. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **Conversions** menu, point to **Standard**, **US to Metric**, **Metric to US**, or **Pitch** and select the type of conversion you want. |
|---|---|---|
