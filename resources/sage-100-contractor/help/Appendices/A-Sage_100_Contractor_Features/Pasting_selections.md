### Pasting selections

You can move text with the **Cut** and **Paste** commands. Identical text can also be inserted elsewhere using the **Copy** and **Paste** commands. After the text is placed on the **Clipboard**, you can continue to paste the text until the next time you **Cut** or **Copy**.

#### To paste a selection:

|  | 1 | Select the text you want to move. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Right-click anywhere in the grid to display the grid menu, then do one of the following: |
|---|---|---|

- Click **Cut**.
- Click **Copy**.

|  | 3 | Insert the pointer where you want to insert the text. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | On the grid menu, click **Paste**. |
|---|---|---|
