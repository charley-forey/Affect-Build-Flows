### Pasting from the calculator

When you use the calculator, you can paste the results to the selected cell or box in the active window.

To paste from the calculator, do one of the following:

- Click the check mark button next to the **Entry**, **Memory**, or **Total** boxes.
- Use the **Copy** and **Paste** commands.
