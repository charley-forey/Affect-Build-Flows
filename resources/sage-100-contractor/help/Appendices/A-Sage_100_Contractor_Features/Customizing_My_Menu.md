### Customizing My Menu

You can customize the My Menu to remove items that you have no interest in or do not use on a regular basis.

1. At the bottom of the My Menu tab, click the **Customize My Menu** button.
2. Clear the check marks from menus or tasks that you do not want to display on your My Menu tab.
3. Click the **Save as My Menu** button to save your adjustments.
