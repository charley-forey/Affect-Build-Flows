### About Quick Lists

With **Quick Lists**, you can create lists of transaction descriptions, names,provinces,postsal codes, and so on, and retrieve the information for entry. Each list is specific to its associated text box and window.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Display a list | Click the blue drop-down arrow next to the text box. Alternatively, select a text box and press the F5 or Shift+? keys. |
| Choose an item | Select the item and double-click. |
| Add an item | Enter the item, and click **Add**. Alternatively, enter the item in the text box and press F5. |
| Delete an item | Select the item in the list, and click **Delete**. |
