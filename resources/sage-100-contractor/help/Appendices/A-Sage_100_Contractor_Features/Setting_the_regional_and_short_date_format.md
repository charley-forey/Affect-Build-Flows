## Configuring Windows Regional and Short Date Format

#### To specify regional formats for Windows:

1. Click the [**Start**] button, point to **Control Panel**, then click **Clock, Language, and Region** .
2. Click **Region and Language**.
3. On the **Formats** tab, select the format for your country.
4. Under **Date and time formats**, from the Short date format drop-down list, select the format used for your country.
5. Click [**OK**].
6. Close the **Clock, Language, and Region** window.
