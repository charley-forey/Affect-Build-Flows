### Deleting all appointments through a certain date

#### To delete all appointments through a certain date:

|  | 1 | Open the calendar. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the last date through which you want to delete appointments. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **Options** menu, click **Delete Appointments**. |
