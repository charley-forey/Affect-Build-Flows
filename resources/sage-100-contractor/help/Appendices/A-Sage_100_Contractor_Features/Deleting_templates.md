### Deleting templates

#### To delete a template:

|  | 1 | On the **File** menu, click **Load/Delete Template**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select the template you want to delete. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Click **Delete**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | Click **Close**. |
|---|---|---|
