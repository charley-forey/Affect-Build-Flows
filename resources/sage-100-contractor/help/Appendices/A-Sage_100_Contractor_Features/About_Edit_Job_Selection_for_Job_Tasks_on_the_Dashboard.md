### About Edit Job Selection for Job Tasks on the Dashboard

The default selection is **List all Contract, Current and Complete jobs**. You can all list jobs by supervisor by selecting the **List jobs for job Supervisor** button. You can select only one job.

If you have more than one content panel for job tasks, you can put a title in the **Selected Job Title** box to differentiate the panels at a quick glance.
