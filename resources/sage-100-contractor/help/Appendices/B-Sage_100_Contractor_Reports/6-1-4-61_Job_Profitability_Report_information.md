### 6-1-4-61 Job Profitability Report information

Important! A client must be entered in the **Client** text box on the **3-5 Jobs (Accounts Receivable)** window for the service receivable invoice to be included in the Service Billing. If you use a specific client in the report selection criteria that is not included on the 3-5 Jobs window, you may get the message “No Matching Records Found.”

Note: Service billing and contract billing do not include PST/QST/HST/GST.
