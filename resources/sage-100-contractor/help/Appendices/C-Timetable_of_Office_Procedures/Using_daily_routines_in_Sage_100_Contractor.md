### Using daily routines in Sage 100 Contractor

We recommend that you perform the following tasks on a daily basis. Be aware, however, that you may need to customize your list of daily accounting tasks to fit your company’s specific needs.

#### To perform daily accounting tasks:

1. Sort and open the daily mail. Write the cost codes on receipts and invoices as necessary.
2. Perform the following **General Ledger** tasks:
   
   1. In the **1-1 Cheques/Bank Charges** window, post handwritten field cheques.
   2. In the **1-2 Deposits/Interest** window, post non job-related deposits.
   3. In the **1-4 Recurring Journal Transactions** window, post recurring journal transactions.
3. Perform the following **Accounts Receivable** tasks:
   
   1. In the **3-2 Receivable Invoices/Credits** window, prepare contract invoices and credits.
   2. File copies awaiting payment.
   3. In the **3-3-1 Cash Receipts** window, deposit and post any cash receipts.
   4. In the **3-3-2 Electronic Receipts** window, enter and post any electronic receipts.
   5. Indicate the job and invoice number on the deposit slip.
   6. In the **3-11 Recurring Receivables** window, post recurring receivables.
4. Perform the following **Service Receivables** tasks:
   
   1. In the **11-2 Work Orders/Invoices/Credits** window, enter work orders and requests for quotes, including new client information.
   2. In the **11-2 Work Orders/Invoices/Credits** window, schedule the work orders.
   3. In the **11-2 Work Orders/Invoices/Credits** window, prepare the service invoices and credits.
   4. In the **11-2 Work Orders/Invoices/Credits** window, post work orders.
   5. File the copies awaiting payment.
   6. In the **3-3-1 Cash Receipts** window, deposit and post any cash receipts. Note: Indicate the client and invoice number on the deposit slip.
   7. In the **3-3-2 Electronic Receipts** window, enter and post any electronic receipts.
5. Perform the following **Accounts Payable** tasks:
   
   1. Stamp and code invoices with vendor, general ledger account, job, and cost code numbers.
   2. In the **4-2 Payable Invoices/Credits** window, post the invoices and credits.
   3. Assign status **2-Review** if sending to the owner or project supervisor for approval.
   4. In the **4-2 Payable Invoices/Credits** window, review the entries with a batch report.
   5. Make duplicate invoices required for time and materials projects and fixed-asset tax information.
   6. File copies awaiting payment.
   7. In the **4-6 Recurring Payables** window, post recurring payables.
6. Perform the following **Daily Payroll** tasks:
   
   1. In the **5-5-1 Daily Payroll Entry** window, enter daily payroll timecards for:
      
      1. Labour
      2. Equipment use.
   2. Print and review the following reports:
      
      1. 5-5-2 Daily Job Report
      2. 5-5-3 Labour Report
      3. 5-5-4 Equipment Report
   3. In the company calendar, enter the sick and vacation days for employees for whom you are not paying for the time off [F2].
7. Make a daily backup of the company data.
