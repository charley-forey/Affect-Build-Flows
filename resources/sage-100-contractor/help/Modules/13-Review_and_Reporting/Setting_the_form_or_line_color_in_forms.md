### Setting the form or line color in forms

Changing the color affects both text and graphics that you draw after the change.

#### To set the form or line color:

|  | 1 | In **13-5 Form/Report Page Design**, on the **Edit** menu, click **Color**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the color you want to use. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Click **OK**. |
|---|---|---|
