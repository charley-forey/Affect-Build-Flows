### Inserting new pages

#### To insert a new page:

|  | 1 | In **13-5 Form/Report Page Design**, display the page above which you want to insert a new page. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | From the **Pages** menu, click **Insert Page**. |
|---|---|---|

Tip: You can also remove pages from the form. [How?](Removing_pages.md)
