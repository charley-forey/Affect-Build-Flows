### Updating scheduled reports

#### To update scheduled reports:

1. On the Sage 100 Contractor desktop, double-click **My Scheduled Reports**.
2. In the **Row**column, click the number of the report you want to update, and then click the **Drill Down**button.
3. When the schedule window opens, enter your Windows password, and click **Save Schedule**.
4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 for every scheduled report.
