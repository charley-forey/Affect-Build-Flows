### Updating the selection criteria in scheduled reports

#### To update the selection criteria in scheduled reports:

Open

**7-5 Scheduled Reports Manager**

.

Click the report you want to update, and then click the

**[Edit Selection Criteria]**

button.

When the window opens, update the selection criteria, and click

**Save**

.

Repeat steps 2 and 3 for every scheduled report.
