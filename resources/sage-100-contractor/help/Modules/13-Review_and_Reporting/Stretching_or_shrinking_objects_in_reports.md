### Stretching or shrinking objects in reports

#### To stretch or shrink an object:

|  | 1 | In **13-3 Report Writer**, open the report in which you want to resize an object. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Drag the item that you want to stretch or shrink. |
|---|---|---|

Important! You cannot resize text. You can only resize the right and bottom edges of an object.
