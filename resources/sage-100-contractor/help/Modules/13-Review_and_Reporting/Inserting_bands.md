### Inserting bands

#### To insert a band:

|  | 1 | In **13-3 Report Writer**, open the report into which you want to insert a band. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the band button immediately below the band where you want to insert the new band. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the toolbar, click the **ins** button. |
|---|---|---|
