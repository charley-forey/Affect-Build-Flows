### Earlier versions of reports created or saved in Version 14

Due to the technology changes in Version 14:

- Version 13 and earlier reports that have been edited or saved in Version 14 will no longer work in Version 13 or earlier releases.
- Reports created in Version 14 will not work in Version 13 or earlier releases.
