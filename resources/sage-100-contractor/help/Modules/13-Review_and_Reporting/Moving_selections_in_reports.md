### Moving objects in reports

#### To move a selection:

|  | 1 | In **13-3 Report Writer**, open the report in which you want to move an object. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select the object you want to move. |
|---|---|---|

Note: To move multiple selections at the same time, hold down the [**Ctrl**] key and drag the mouse over the objects to be selected.

|  | 3 | Drag the selection to the desired location. |
|---|---|---|
