### Deleting report schedules

Important! A report schedule can only be deleted through **7-5 Scheduled Reports Manager**. You cannot delete a report schedule in the **Report Scheduling** window.

#### To delete a report schedule:

|  | 1 | Open **7-5 Scheduled Reports Manager**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select the scheduled item by clicking on its row. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Click **[Delete]**. |
