### Deleting bands

#### To delete a band:

|  | 1 | In **13-3 Report Writer**, open the report from which you want to delete a band. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the band button of the band you want to delete. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the toolbar, click the **del** button. |
