### Adjusting the proportions of pictures in reports

#### To adjust the proportions of a picture:

|  | 1 | In **13-3 Report Writer**, open the report in which you want to re-proportion a graphic. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Drag the picture in the direction that you want to stretch or shrink it. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Click the image to change its proportions. |
|---|---|---|
