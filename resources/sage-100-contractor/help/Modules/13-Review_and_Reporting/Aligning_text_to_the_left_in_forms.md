### Aligning text to the left in forms

#### To align text to the left:

|  | 1 | In **13-5 Report/Form Page Design**, open the form in which you want to align text. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the text that you want to align. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | From the **Edit** menu, click **Align Left**. |
|---|---|---|

Note: The text aligns itself to the left where the text begins, and not the left margin.
