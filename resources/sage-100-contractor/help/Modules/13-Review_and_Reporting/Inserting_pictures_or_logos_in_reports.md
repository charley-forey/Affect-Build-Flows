### Inserting pictures or logos in reports

#### To insert a picture or logo:

|  | 1 | In **13-3 Form/Report Page Design**, open the report into which you want to insert a graphic. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the **Insert** menu, click **Picture**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | The **Assign Picture** dialog box appears. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | Select the bitmap file that you want to insert. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | Click **Open**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 6 | On the **Edit** menu, click **Select Objects**, then click the picture and move to the appropriate area on the report. |
|---|---|---|

Tip: You can resize a picture or logo larger or smaller and [retain its original proportions](Adjusting_the_proportions_of_pictures_in_reports.md).
