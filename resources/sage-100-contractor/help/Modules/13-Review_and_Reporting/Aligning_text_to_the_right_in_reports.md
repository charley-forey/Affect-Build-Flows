### Aligning text to the right in reports

#### To align text to the right:

|  | 1 | In **13-3 Report Writer**, open the report in which you want to align text. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the text field that you want to align. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **Edit** menu, click **Align Right**. |
|---|---|---|

Note: The text aligns itself to the furthest right where the text ends, and not the right margin.
