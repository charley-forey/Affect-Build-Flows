### Voiding records in 1-3 Journal Transactions

Records in **1-3 Journal Transactions** can have a status of **1-Open** or **2-Cleared**.

Important! Best accounting practices require that you do not void transactions that have been processed by the bank. Therefore, it is not possible to void transactions with a status of **2-Cleared**.

It is possible to void records with a status of **1-Open**. However, the program only allows you to void records from within their original posting period.
