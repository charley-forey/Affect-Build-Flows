### Assigning departments

#### To assign departments:

|  | 1 | Open **1-9 Company Departments**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the menu bar, click **Update Accounts**. |
|---|---|---|

Tip: If a ledger account is not set up to use departments, Sage 100 Contractor does not create any departments for that ledger account.
