### Recalling trial reconciliations

#### To recall a trial reconciliation:

|  | 1 | Open **1-5 Bank Reconciliation**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | In the **Account#** box, enter the bank account number. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Click the **Display** button. |
|---|---|---|

Tip: When you save a trial reconciliation, the next time you recall the reconciliation, the cheques and deposits that were cleared still display with a **Clear** status. However, any new transactions appear with an **Open** status. The computed balance will be the same. To start the reconciliation over again, clear out the trial reconciliation. [How?](Clearing_trial_reconciliations.md)
