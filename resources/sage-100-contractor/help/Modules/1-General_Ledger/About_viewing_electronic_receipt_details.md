# About viewing electronic receipt details

You use the **Electronic Receipts** detail window to:

- View the details of a selected electronic receipt that you have posted to your ledger. You can view details for electronic receipts that were processed through Sage Exchange or entered manually in Sage 100 Contractor for reference purposes.
- Print a receipt for your client.

[![Closed](http://sage100contractorhelp.sagecre.com/help/sage100contractor/US/20_5/Skins/Default/Stylesheets/Images/transparent.gif)To view the details of an electronic receipt](javascript:void(0);)

|  | 1 | In the **1-3 Journal Transaction**window, display the receipt transaction. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | From the Options menu, click View Electronic Receipt. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | To print a receipt for your client, click the Print receipt link. |
|---|---|---|
