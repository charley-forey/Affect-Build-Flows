### Verification tasks for year end

After finishing the year-end process, you can verify the process was successful by doing the following tasks.

#### Verification tasks in the new company

![](http://sage100contractorhelp.sagecre.com/help/sage100contractor/US/20_5/Content/Resources/Images/check_box_unchecked.PNG) Open **3-5 Jobs (Accounts Receivable)**, and verify that the jobs you selected to be removed have been removed.

![](http://sage100contractorhelp.sagecre.com/help/sage100contractor/US/20_5/Content/Resources/Images/check_box_unchecked.PNG) Open **5-2-2 Payroll Records**, select **File** > **Count** and verify that you find zero records.
