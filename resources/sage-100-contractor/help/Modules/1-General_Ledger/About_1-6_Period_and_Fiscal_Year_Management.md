### About 1-6 Period and Fiscal Year Management

The **1-6 Period and Fiscal Year Management**window provides options for advancing to the next fiscal year and archiving data from your previous fiscal accounting year. You use this window to advance from one fiscal period to the next.

If you are on period 12 of your current fiscal year, use this window to advance to the first period of the next fiscal year.

Tip: Period 1 of the next fiscal year is available while you are still on your current year. You can begin entering transactions for period 1 of the next fiscal year by selecting the next available fiscal year from the [Posting Period window](Change_Posting_Periods.md).Periods 2-12 become available when you advance to the next fiscal year by clicking [**Change Period**].

See [1-6 Period and Fiscal Year Management](1-6_Period_and_Fiscal_Year_Management.md) for more information about the options in this window.

Note: This window replaces the "close year" function in previous releases. Beginning with version 19.2, Sage 100 Contractor no longer requires you to close your fiscal year.
