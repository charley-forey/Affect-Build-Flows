### About the Audit History window

You open the Audit History window from **1-6 Period and Fiscal Year Management** by way of the **View Audit History** link located at the bottom-left of the window. Every time you initiate an audit, the program logs audit history in the window’s grid.

The **Audit History** window displays three columns, **Date**, **User Name**, and **Audit Results**. The results displayed can be:

**Audit successful.** No errors were found.

**Repair successful.** Errors were found and repaired when the balances were recalculated.

**[n] errors detected.** A number of errors that couldn’t be repaired and were logged.

The **Audit History** window also provides menu commands for saving your audit history in several file formats and for printing the audit history file.
