## Calendar year-end checklist—Payroll and T5018 forms

#### Introduction

Important! Use this checklist only if your fiscal year-end is **not** December 31. If your fiscal year ends on December 31, use [this checklist](Combined_fiscal_year-end_and_calendar_year-end_checklist.md) instead.

Allow sufficient time to proceed through this checklist in its entirety for each company. The length of time it takes to complete each company will vary. If you have multiple companies, perform these steps from start to finish for each company separately.

The following are important items to take into consideration:

- Payroll must be closed before new year payroll is processed. If new payroll is entered, year-end reports will show incorrect data.
- You must create a separate archive file for Payroll (separate from the general ledger archive)and T5018 information.

Follow this process in the order items are listed. Not following the process in order can result in an improper close or a damaged archive file, in addition to incorrect balances on T5018 forms and T-4s. If payroll is closed in the wrong company, there could inadvertently be two years of payroll records in the same company instead of just one year.
