### About payable invoice types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| 1-Contract | Use 1-Contract when the invoice affects the billing for the referenced subcontract. A payable invoice with this type increases the vendor balance, but a credit invoice assigned this type reduces the vendor balance. |
| 2-Memo | Use 2-Memo when the invoice does not affect the billing for the referenced subcontract. |
