### Paying vendors by EFT payment

You can pay a vendor by an EFT payment file if the company and vendor have been set up with the required information in both **7-1 Company Information** Vendor EFT Payment Setup tab and **4-4 Vendors (Accounts Payable)** EFT Payment Setup tab. [How?](Adding_ACH_payment_information_for_vendors.md)

Note: You cannot mix payment types when you use **4-3-2 Pay Vendors**, you must do the payment types in groups.

#### To pay by EFT payment:

|  | 1 | Open **4-3-2 Pay Vendors**.<br>Invoices that you have selected using **4-3-1 Set Invoices to Pay** appear in the grid area. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select invoices to pay that have **Yes** in the **EFT Available** column. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Click the **Pay by**arrow, and then select pay by **EFT Payment**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | Click [**Pay Vendors**]. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | On the **Vendor EFT Payment File Creator** window, enter the appropriate **Transaction Date**, **Deposit Date**, and then click the [**Create File**] button. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 6 | Click the [**Save**] button to save the direct deposit file. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 7 | Click [**OK**]. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 8 | Do one of the following: |
|---|---|---|

- Click [**Yes**] to print and email the **4-3-7-21 EFT Payment Receipt** report.
- click [**No**] to exit.

|  | 9 | If you clicked [**Yes**], click the Print toolbar button. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 10 | Click [**Yes**] to send the emails to those listed for emailing and print the **4-3-7-21 EFT Payment Receipt** for those without an email address, or click [**No**] to send all of the EFT Payment Receipts to the printer. |
|---|---|---|

> Note: If you are sending through Microsoft Outlook and a message is displayed about a program sending an email message on your behalf, click [**Allow**].

|  | 11 | If you clicked [**Yes**] in step 10, you can also print the EFT Payment Receipts that were emailed by clicking [**Yes**] to print the emailed payments receipts, or click [**No**] to exit, which will just print the EFT payment receipts for those that were not emailed. |
|---|---|---|
