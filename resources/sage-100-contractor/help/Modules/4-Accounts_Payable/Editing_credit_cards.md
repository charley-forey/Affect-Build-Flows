### Editing credit cards

You can edit the **Description** information for a credit card; however, you cannot change the credit card subaccount number.

#### To edit a credit card:

|  | 1 | Open **4-7-2 Add/Edit Credit Card**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the drop-down arrow with **Select Card Issuer Account** and select an account. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Click **Edit Card**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | Edit the description. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | Click **Save**. |
|---|---|---|
