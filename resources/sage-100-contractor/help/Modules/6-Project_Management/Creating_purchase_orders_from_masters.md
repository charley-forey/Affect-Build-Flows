### Creating purchase orders from masters

#### To create a purchase order from a master:

|  | 1 | Open **6-6-1 Purchase Orders**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select the master purchase order. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | In the **Current** column, enter the part quantities you want to order at this time. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | On the **Options** menu, click **Create New Order**. |
|---|---|---|

Sage 100 Contractor automatically creates the purchase order, assigns a record number, and displays it.
