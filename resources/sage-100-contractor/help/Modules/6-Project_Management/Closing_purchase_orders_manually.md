### Closing purchase orders manually

When the sum of the **To Date** and **Cancelled** columns equal the sum of the **Quantity** column, close the purchase order.

#### To manually close a purchase order:

|  | 1 | Open **6-6-1 Purchase Orders**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select the purchase order. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | In the **Status** list, click **4-Closed**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | On the **File** menu, click **Save**. |
|---|---|---|
