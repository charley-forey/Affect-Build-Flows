### Creating purchase orders from change orders

Important! The change order **Status** must be **1-Approved** to use this function.

#### To create a purchase order from a change order:

|  | 1 | Open **6-4-1 Change Orders**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select the change order. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **Options** menu, click **Create Purchase Order**. |
|---|---|---|
