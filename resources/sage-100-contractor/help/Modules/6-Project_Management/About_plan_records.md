### About plan records

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the [Document Control Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/project-management).

In the **6-11-5 Plan Records** window, you can enter and track the revisions made to plans. In addition, you can track who has received the plans.
