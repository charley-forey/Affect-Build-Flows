### Deleting Cost to Complete reports

#### To delete a Cost to Complete report:

|  | 1 | Open **6-8-1 Cost to Complete**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Using the data control, select the record. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **Edit** menu, click **Delete Cost/Complete**. |
|---|---|---|
