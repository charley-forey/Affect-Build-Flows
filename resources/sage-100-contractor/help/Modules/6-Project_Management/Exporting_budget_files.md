### Exporting budget files

#### To export a budget file:

|  | 1 | Open **6-2 Budgets**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the **Options** menu, point to **Export**, then click **Budget File**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Name the file, then click **Save**. |
|---|---|---|

Note: Files are specific to the company in which you save them.
