### Reviewing subcontract change orders

You can review a summary of change orders for subcontracts that are not rejected or void.

#### To review a summary of change orders for a contract:

|  | 1 | Open **6-7-1 Subcontracts**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select the subcontract. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **Options** menu, click **Changes**. |
