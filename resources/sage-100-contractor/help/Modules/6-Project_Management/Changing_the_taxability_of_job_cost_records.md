### Changing the taxability of job cost records

When you compute invoices, sales taxes for job costs are calculated based on whether the cost type is taxable. In the job record, you can indicate which cost types are taxable.

#### To change the taxability of a job cost record:

|  | 1 | Open **6-3 Job Costs**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Using the data control, select the record. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Select or clear the **Subject to sales tax** check box. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | Click the **Override Billing Amount** check box. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | On the **File** menu, click **Save**. |
|---|---|---|
