### Viewing lists of change orders for jobs

You can view a list of the prime contract change orders.

#### To view a list of change orders for a job:

|  | 1 | Open **3-5 Jobs (Accounts Receivable)**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the **Options** menu, click **Change Orders**. |
