### About inventory status

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the [Inventory Add-On Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management).

The status of an inventory record indicates where it is in the process.

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| 1-Open | Indicates the inventory is available. |
| 2-Review | Indicates the management or bookkeeping staff should review the record before shipping. |
| 3-Hold Stock | Indicates inventory reserved for a job or work order. When you assign an inventory record this status, Sage 100 Contractor reduces the quantity of stock that is available. |
| 4-Shipped | Indicates that the inventory was sent to a job or reserved for a work order. When you assign an inventory record this status, Sage 100 Contractor reduces the quantity of stock that is available and the quantity of stock on hand. |
