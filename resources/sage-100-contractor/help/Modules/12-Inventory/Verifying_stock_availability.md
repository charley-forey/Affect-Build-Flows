### Verifying stock availability

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the [Inventory Add-On Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management).

Important! Selecting this command in the **11-2 Work Orders/Invoices/Credits** window does not verify stock for status **7-Work Order**.

When you select the **Verify Stock on Save** command, Sage 100 Contractor makes certain that you have enough stock in the specified inventory location. If there is not enough stock available, Sage 100 Contractor warns you.

To activate the verify stock on save feature, on the **Options** menu, click **Verify Stock on Save**.
