### Account status

Note: Account status is set by the payroll administrator, not provided by the employee.

| Status | Definition |
|---|---|
| 0-None | The account is assigned no status. |
| 1-Active | When active, the payroll information will be included in the direct deposit file with its respective dollar amounts. |
| 2-Inactive | The account is inactive. |
