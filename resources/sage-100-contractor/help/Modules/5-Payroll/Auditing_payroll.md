### Auditing payroll

When the audit is complete, print the **Payroll Audit Report**. The audit report lists all errors uncovered during the audit, regardless of whether you have already resolved them.

#### To audit the payroll:

|  | 1 | Open **5-3-7 Payroll Audit**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the **Audit** button. |
