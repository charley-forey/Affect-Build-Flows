### Checking for budgeted cost codes and cost types

When you select the **Budget Verification** command, Sage 100 Contractor compares the cost codes and cost types in the timecard against the cost codes and cost types in the budgets. When you save the record, Sage 100 Contractor notifies you if a line item does not appear in the budget of a job.

#### To check for budgeted cost codes and cost types:

Open

**5-2-2 Payroll Records**

.

On the

**Options**

menu, click

**Budget Verification**

.
