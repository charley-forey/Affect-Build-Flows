### Creating timecards for daily payroll

To begin processing payroll, convert the daily payroll records into timecards. You can then review the timecards in the **5-2-2 Payroll Records**window.

#### To create timecards:

|  | 1 | Open **5-5-1 Daily Payroll Entry**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the **Options** menu, click **Timecards**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | In the **Beginning Date** box, enter the date of the first daily record you want to convert. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | In the **Ending Date** box, enter the date of the last daily record you want to convert. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | In the **Payroll Quarter** box, enter the payroll quarter. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 6 | Click **OK**. |
|---|---|---|

Notes:

- When you create timecards, Sage 100 Contractor posts the costs for equipment time to the **General Ledger** and creates the job or equipment cost records.
- When you final-compute payroll, Sage 100 Contractor posts the costs for labour to the **General Ledger** and creates the job cost records.
