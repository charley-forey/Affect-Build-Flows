### Rate

The rate can be assigned a percentage number or a dollar amount, depending on which **Rate Type** is selected. If the total percent of net equals 100, a **3-Remainder of cheque** line is not required.

Important! When a line is set to **3-Remainder of cheque**, the **Amount/Rate** cell is disabled. The remainder of the cheque will automatically be determined by Sage 100 Contractor and put into the designated account.
