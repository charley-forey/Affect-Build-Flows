### Deleting schedules

The notes in a schedule about project delays can prove useful if disputes arise. It is a good idea to retain a job’s schedule with the other job records. When the books are closed for the fiscal year, job records assigned status **6-Closed**, and the associated records, including the schedule, are deleted.

#### To delete a schedule:

|  | 1 | Open **10-3 Schedules**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Using the data control, select the record. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **Edit** menu, click **Delete Schedule**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | Under **Removal Method**, select what you want to delete. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | Click **OK**. |
|---|---|---|
