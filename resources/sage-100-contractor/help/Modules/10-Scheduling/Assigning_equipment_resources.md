### Assigning equipment resources

#### To assign equipment resources:

|  | 1 | Open **10-3 Schedules**. In the data control box, enter the job number. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | In the **Phase** list, click the phase. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | In the **Task#** column, select the task(s) to which you want to assign equipment. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | On the **Options** menu, click **Equipment**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | In the **Equipment** cell, do one of the following: |
|---|---|---|

- Enter the equipment number.
- Right-click the cell to open the **Lookup** window, then select the equipment.

|  | 6 | In the **Start Time** cell, enter the time you expect the equipment to begin. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 7 | In the **End Time** cell, enter the time you expect the equipment to finish. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 8 | Repeat steps 5–7 for each piece of equipment you want to assign to the task. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 9 | On the **File** menu, click **Save**. |
|---|---|---|
