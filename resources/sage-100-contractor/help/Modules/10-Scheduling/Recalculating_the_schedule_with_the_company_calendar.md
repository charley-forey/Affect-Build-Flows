### Recalculating the schedule with the company calendar

When you select the **Add off days from company calendar** check box, Sage 100 Contractor reviews the company calendar and updates the schedule when you recalculate.

#### To recalculate the schedule with the company calendar:

|  | 1 | Open **10-3 Schedules**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select a schedule. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Select the **Add off days from company calendar** check box. |
