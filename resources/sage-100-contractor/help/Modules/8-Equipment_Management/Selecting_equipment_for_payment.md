### Selecting equipment for payment

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the **[Equipment Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management)**.

#### To select equipment for payment:

|  | 1 | Open **8-5 Equipment Payments**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select the equipment, and click the **Pay** button. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **File** menu, click **Print Cheques**. |
|---|---|---|

Note: When you print an equipment cheque, Sage 100 Contractor decreases the loan balance by the cheque amount and advances the due date.
