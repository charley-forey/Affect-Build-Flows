### Entering historical equipment revenue records

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the **[Equipment Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management)**.

When billing for time and materials, Sage 100 Contractor uses the billing rates in place of the cost recovery rates and does not create cost and revenue records.

#### To enter a historical equipment revenue record:

|  | 1 | Open **8-7 Equipment Revenue**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | In the **Equipment** text box, enter the equipment number. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | In the **Trans#** text box, type Start Up. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | In the **Date** text box, enter the date. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | In the **Description** text box, type **Start Up**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 6 | In the **Cost Code** text box, enter the cost code. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 7 | In the **Revenue** text box, enter the revenue generated by the equipment. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 8 | On the **File** menu, click **Save**. |
|---|---|---|
