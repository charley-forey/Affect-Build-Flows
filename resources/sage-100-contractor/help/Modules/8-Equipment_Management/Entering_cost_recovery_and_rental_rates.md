### Entering cost recovery and billing rates

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the **[Equipment Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management)**.

Before you start, use the **Cost Recovery Worksheet** tab in the **8-3 Equipment** window to help you determine appropriate cost recovery and billing rates.

Use the **Cost and Billing Rates** tab to enter cost recovery rates and billing rates to charge projects for **Operated**, **Idled**, and **Standby** equipment costs.

#### To enter cost recovery and billing rates:

1. Open **8-3 Equipment**.
2. Using the data control, select the equipment.
3. On the **Cost and Billing Rates** tab:
   
   1. Under **Cost Rates**, enter the rates at which you recover costs for equipment that is operated, idled, or standing by. These rates can differ from the **Calculated Cost** rates.
   2. Under **Billing Rates**, enter the hourly or per mile billing rates.
   3. Under **Daily**, enter the billing rates per day.
   4. Under **Weekly**, enter the billing rates per week.
   5. Under **Monthly**, enter the billing rates per month.
   6. In the **Mobilization Billing Rate** box, enter the flat rate to charge for transporting the equipment to and from a job site.
4. On the **File** menu, click **Save**.
