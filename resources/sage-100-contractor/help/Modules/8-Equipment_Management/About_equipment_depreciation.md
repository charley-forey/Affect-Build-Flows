### About equipment depreciation

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the **[Equipment Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management)**.

In the **8-6 Equipment Depreciation** window, you can post the depreciation for equipment. You can change or delete the amount to depreciate, and you can post depreciation to specific pieces of equipment.

When you save a record in **8-6 Equipment Depreciation**, Sage 100 Contractor posts the depreciation for each piece of equipment with the amount in the **Depreciation** cell.
