### Posting equipment depreciation

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the **[Equipment Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management)**.

#### To post equipment depreciation:

|  | 1 | Open **8-6 Equipment Depreciation**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the **Options** menu, click **Post Depreciation**. |
|---|---|---|

When the depreciation posts, a message window appears confirming that the depreciation has been posted.

Tip: You can change the amount of depreciation for a piece of equipment. Select the equipment, and enter the new amount in the **Depreciation** cell.
