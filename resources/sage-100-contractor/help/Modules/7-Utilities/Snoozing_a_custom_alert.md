### Snoozing a custom alert

You can snooze a custom alert to skip the processing of the alert.

The available day options for snoozing are:

- 1 day
- 2 days
- 3 days
- 4 days
- 5 days
- 6 days
- 7 days
- 14 days
- 30 days

Snoozing an alert query changes the status to Snoozed and updates the Snoozed Until field with the appropriate date.

#### To snooze an alert query:

|  | 1 | Open **7-6 Alerts Manager**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Select alert query to snooze. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Click [**Snooze**] and select the days for snoozing. |
|---|---|---|
