### Update Sage 100 Contractor contact records

If Sage 100 Contractor detects conflicts when attempting to sync with Outlook, you can update just the Outlook contact information, or update both the Outlook contact information and the Sage 100 Contractor contact records.

#### To update Sage 100 Contractor contact records:

Click the Update

Sage 100 Contractor

contact records button next to the Full Name field.

Note: If you do not click the Update Sage 100 Contractor contact records check box when correcting contact record conflicts, the conflict may be presented again the next time you synchronize records with Outlook.
