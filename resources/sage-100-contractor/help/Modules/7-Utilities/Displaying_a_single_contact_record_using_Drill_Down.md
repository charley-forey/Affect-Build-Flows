### Displaying a single contact record using Drill Down

#### To drill down a contact record:

|  | 1 | Click a single row or click any column within that row. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Click the Drill Down on the bottom left of the window. |
|---|---|---|

The source contact record data entry screen for the currently selected row is opened in the appropriate window. For example, clicking on a row with a job contact type drills down to the contact record in the **3-5 Jobs (Accounts Receivable)** window.
