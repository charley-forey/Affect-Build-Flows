### Installing printers

Operating system manufactures and printer manufacturers provide printer drivers, not Sage 100 Contractor. To print more than simple text requires printer drivers specific to your brand and model of printer. Generic text-only printer drivers work with all printers, though they cannot print graphics.

We recommend using laser or ink jet printers with Sage 100 Contractor because they are able to print graphics. You can also use dot matrix printers for producing accounting reports and cheques.

#### To install printers:

|  | 1 | On the **Start** menu, point to **Settings**, and click **Printers**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | Double-click the **Add Printers** icon. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | Follow the instructions in the **Add Printer Wizard**. |
|---|---|---|

Note: Refer to your operating system’s documentation for more information about installing printers.
