# About the Electronic Receipt detail window

You can use the **3-3-2 Electronic Receipts** window to record an electronic receipt that you have already processed online using Sage Virtual Terminal or another payment services provider.

When you click **Post Only** to update your Sage 100 Contractor ledger, the **Electronic Receipt** detail window appears. You use it to record the details for the receipt that you processed online, so that later you can reconcile your ledger with your Sage Payment Solutions account statement.

When you finish entering the details, click **Save** to finish posting.

Tip: Use the **1-3 Journal Transaction window** to review the posted transaction, including the details of the electronic receipt, and print a receipt for your client.
