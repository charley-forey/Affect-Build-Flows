### Changing the billing status of job cost records

#### To change the billing status of a job cost record:

|  | 1 | Open **6-3 Job Costs**, and using the data control, select a job cost record. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | From the **Time and Materials Billing** section of the window, in the **Billing Status** list, click the new billing status. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | On the **File** menu, click **Save**. |
|---|---|---|
