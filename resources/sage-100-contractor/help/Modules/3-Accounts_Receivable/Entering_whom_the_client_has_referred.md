### Entering whom the client has referred

In the **Referred** window, you can note who the client has referred to your company.

#### To enter whom the client has referred:

|  | 1 | Open **3-6 Receivable Clients**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the **Options** menu, point to **Referrals**, then click **Referred**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | In the **Referred** cell, enter the client number of the client who was referred to your company. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | In the **Date** cell, enter the date of the referral. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 5 | In the **Sale (Y/N)** cell, type Y if the referral resulted in a sale, or N if the referral did not result in a sale. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 6 | Repeat steps 3–5 for each referral. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 7 | On the **File** menu, click **Save**. |
|---|---|---|
