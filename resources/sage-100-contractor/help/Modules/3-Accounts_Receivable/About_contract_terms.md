### About contract terms

Important! When entering the discount date and due date for a job, you must also indicate the billing cycle.

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| Finance Rate | Enter the finance rate to apply when payments are late. |
| Holdback Rate | Enter the percent of the entire contract withheld as holdback. When you enter an invoice, Sage 100 Contractor automatically calculates holdback for the invoice. |
| Discount Rate | Enter the discount rate. |
| Due Date | Indicates the date payment is due. |
| Discount Terms | Indicates the date by which payment is due for the client to receive a discount. |
| Contract Amount | Indicates the total amount of the contract. |

Tip: If you have indicated a client number in the job record, Sage 100 Contractor uses the term rates from the client record.
