### Assigning status 5-Complete to groups of jobs

#### To assign status 5-Complete to a group of jobs:

|  | 1 | Open **3-5 Jobs (Accounts Receivable)**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the **Update** menu, click **Completed Status**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 3 | In the **Picklist** window, select the jobs to which you want to assign status **5-Complete**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 4 | Click the check mark button. |
|---|---|---|
