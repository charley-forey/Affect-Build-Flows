# About the Sage Exchange Vault

Sage Exchange is a secure, PCI-compliant, payment-processing application that integrates Sage 100 Contractor with Sage Payment Solutions.

Sage Exchange stores credit card details securely in an online vault system, allowing you to save and reuse credit card information for your clients without storing it in your company database. The Sage Exchange Vault keeps your client data secure, ensuring that your receipt processing is PCI compliant and reducing your exposure to credit card fraud.
