### Automatically setting or clearing PST status for material parts in a takeoff

You can set or clear the PST tax status automatically for material parts in a takeoff.

1. In the **9-5 Takeoffs** window, open a takeoff.
2. On the **Update** menu, do one of the following:
   
   - Point to **Grid**, and then click **Set Material Parts as Subject to PST** or **Clear the Subject to PST**.
   - Point to **Takeoff**, and then click **Set Material Parts as Subject to PST** or **Clear the Subject to PST**.
