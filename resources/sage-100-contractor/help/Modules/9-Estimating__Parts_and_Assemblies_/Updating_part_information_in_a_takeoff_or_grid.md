### Updating part information in a takeoff or grid

This functionality is only available if you have purchased the [Estimating Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/estimating).

Important! You must type and between two numbers in the **Value** text box when using the **between** operator.

Before saving, review the changes. After you save, you cannot reverse the update.

#### To update part information in a takeoff or grid:

1. Open a takeoff in **9-5 Takeoffs**.
2. On the **Update** menu, do one of the following:
   
   - Point to **Grid**, and then click **Grid Parts**.
   - Point to **Takeoff**, and then click **Takeoff Parts**.
3. Under **Search Fields**:
   
   1. In the **Field** list, click the type of information for which you are searching.
   2. In the **Operator** list, click the math operator.
   3. In the **Value** text box, enter the value for which you are searching.
4. Under **Change Fields**:
   
   1. In the **Field** list, click the type of information that you want to update.
   2. In the **Type** list, click how you want to update the data.
   3. In the **Value** text box, enter the value that you want to apply.
5. Click **Update**.
