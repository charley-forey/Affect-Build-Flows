### Updating part costs

This functionality is available only if you have purchased the [Estimating Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/estimating).

You can update part costs in a takeoff grid or an entire takeoff. When you update by part cost, Sage 100 Contractor updates the costs using the last cost amount from the parts database.

Important! Before saving, review the changes. After you save, you cannot reverse the update.

#### To update part costs:

|  | 1 | Open a takeoff in **9-5 Takeoffs**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | To update a specific grid, do the following. |
|---|---|---|

1. In the **Bid Item** list, select the bid item.
2. In the **Phase** list, select the phase.

|  | 3 | Do one of the following: |
|---|---|---|

- On the **Update** menu, point to **Grid**, point to **Prices from Parts Database**, and click **Use Default Cost**.
- On the **Update** menu, point to **Takeoff**, point to **Prices from Parts Database**, and click **Use Default Cost**.
