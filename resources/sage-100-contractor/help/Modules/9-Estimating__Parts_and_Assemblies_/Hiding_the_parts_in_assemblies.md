### Hiding the parts in assemblies

Complete functionality is only available if you have purchased the [Estimating Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/estimating).

Assembly functionality is only available if you have purchased the [Estimating Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/estimating) or [Service Receivables Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management).

#### To hide the parts in assemblies:

|  | 1 | Open **9-5 Takeoffs**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the **Options** menu, click **Hide Assembly Parts**. |
