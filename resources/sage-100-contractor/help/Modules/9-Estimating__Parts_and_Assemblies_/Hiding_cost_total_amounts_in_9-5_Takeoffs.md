### Hiding cost total amounts in 9-5 Takeoffs

This functionality is available only if you have purchased the [Estimating Add-On Module](http://sagemasterbuilder.com/products/p_f_est_estimating.asp).

In business situations where certain amounts and totals must be hidden from view, Sage 100 Contractor provides the functionality to make sure that your company profit and margin information is kept private. Pressing the F7 key when your cursor is in almost any text box, list, check box, grid cell, or column opens the **Field Properties** window. Using the **Field Properties** window, a company administrator can set up the security in the **9-5 Takeoffs** window to hide or reveal cost total amounts.

Important!

- The company administrator may want to hide some of these items and reveal others, but to be able to view **9-5 Takeoffs** from a cost-only perspective, all the items should be hidden.
- If a report contains a field that is restricted for a user, then that report is unavailable to that user.

#### To hide cost total amounts:

1. In the **9-5 Takeoffs** window, click the **Takeoff Details** tab, and then:
   
   1. In the grid, select the **Ext Cost** column, press F7, and then set the security.
   2. Select the **Cost** column, and then press F7.
   3. Select the **Formula** column, and then press F7.
   4. Select the **O/H Rate**column, and then press F7.
   5. Select the **Overhead**column, and then press F7.
   6. Select the **Profit Rate**column, and then press F7.
   7. Select the **Profit**column, and then press F7.
2. Click the **Insurance/Tax/Bonding** tab, and then under **Liability Insurance**:
   
   1. Click in the **Subcontract Amount** cell, press F7, and then set the security.
   2. Click in the **Payroll Amount** cell, press F7, and then set the security.
3. Click the **Bid Summary** tab, click in the **Gross Margin Override** cell, press F7, and then set the security.
4. On the menu bar, point to **Update**> **Grid** (or **Takeoff**) > **Overhead/Profit by Cost Type**.
5. In the **Overhead/Profit by Cost Type** window, click in each cell in the **Overhead %** and **Profit %** columns, press F7, and then set the security.
6. Close the **Overhead/Profit by Cost Type** window. Do not update.
7. In the **Bid Items** window grid:
   
   1. Click the **Takeoff Details** tab.
   2. Next to the **Bid Items** cell, click the detail button.
   3. Select the **Overhead** and **Profit** columns, press F7, and then set the security.
