### About the check boxes in the 9-2 Parts window

The complete parts and assembly functionality is only available if you have purchased the [Estimating Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/estimating).

The Inventory functionality is only available if you have the [Inventory Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management).

The Service Receivables functionality is only available if you have purchased the [Service Receivables Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management).

#### General Information tab

- **Subject to PST:** Sets material parts as taxable by default when you import or export them in the 9-5 Takeoffs windows.

#### Inventory tab

- **Stock Item:**Indicates the part is normally carried in your inventory.
- **Serialized Item:**Indicates the part uses a serial number. When you receive parts, Sage 100 Contractor requires you to provide the serial number for each part.
- **Required from Inv.:**Requires you to enter a service location on the work order or invoice.

#### Service Equipment tab

- **Service Equipment Item:**Adds the part to a client’s list of service equipment in the client record.
