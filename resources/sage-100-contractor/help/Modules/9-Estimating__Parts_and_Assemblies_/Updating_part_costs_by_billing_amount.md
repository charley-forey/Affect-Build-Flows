### Updating part costs by billing amount

Note: This functionality is available only if you have the [Estimating Module](http://www.na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/estimating).

Caution! Always make a backup of your data before you update prices or add parts.

Important:

Before saving, review the changes. After you save, you cannot reverse the update.

#### To update the part cost by billing amount:

|  | 1 | Open a takeoff in **9-5 Takeoffs**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | To update a specific grid: |
|---|---|---|

1. In the **Bid Item** list, select the bid item.
2. In the **Phase** list, select the phase.

|  | 3 | Do one of the following: |
|---|---|---|

- On the **Update** menu, point to **Grid**, then point to **Prices from Parts Database**, then click **Use Part Billing Amount**.
- On the **Update** menu, point to **Takeoff**, point to **Prices from Parts Database**, then click **Use Part Billing Amount**.
