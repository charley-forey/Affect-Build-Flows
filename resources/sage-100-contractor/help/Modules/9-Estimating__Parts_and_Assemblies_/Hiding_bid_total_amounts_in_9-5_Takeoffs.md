### Hiding bid total amounts in 9-5 Takeoffs

This functionality is only available if you have purchased the [Estimating Add-On Module](http://sagemasterbuilder.com/products/p_f_est_estimating.asp).

In business situations where certain amounts and totals must be hidden from view, Sage 100 Contractor provides the functionality to make sure that your company bid information is kept private. Pressing the F7 key when your cursor is in almost any cell, list, check box, grid cell, or column opens the **Field Properties** window. In the **Field Properties** window, a company administrator can set up the security on the **9-5 Takeoffs** window to hide or reveal bid total amounts.

Important!

- The company administrator may want to hide some of these items and reveal others, but to be able to view **9-5 Takeoffs** from a cost-only perspective, all the items should be hidden.
- If a report contains a field that is restricted for a user, then that report is unavailable to that user.

#### To hide bid total amounts:

1. In the **9-5 Takeoffs** window, click the **Takeoff Details** tab, then:
   
   1. In the grid, select the **O/H Rate** column, press F7, and then set the security.
   2. Select the **Overhead** column, and then press F7.
   3. Select the **Profit Rate** column, and then press F7.
   4. Select the **Profit**column, and then press F7.
   5. Select the **Ext Price**columnl, and then press F7.
2. Click the **Insurance/Tax/Bonding** tab, and then:.
   
   1. Under **Liability Insurance**, in the **Bid** line, place your cursor in the **Amount** cell, press F7, and then set the security.
   2. Under **Use Tax**, in the **Bid** line, place your cursor in the **Amount** cell, press F7, and then set the security.
   3. Under **Bonding**, in the **Limit 1** line, place your cursor in the **Amount** cell, and then press F7.
   4. Under **Bonding**, in the **Limit 2** line, place your cursor in the **Amount** cell, and then press F7.
   5. Under **Bonding**, in the **Limit 3** line, place your cursor in the **Amount** cell, and then press F7.
3. Click the **Bid Summary** tab, and then:
   
   1. Place your cursor in the **Gross Margin Override** text box, press F7, and then set the security.
   2. Place your cursor in the **Bid Amount** text box, and then press F7.
   3. Place your cursor in the **Markup %** text boxl, and then press F7.
4. On the menu bar, point to **Update** > **Grid** (or **Takeoff**) > **Overhead/Profit by Cost Type**.
5. In the **Overhead/Profit by Cost Type** window, click in each cell in the **Overhead %** and **Profit %** columns, press F7, and set the security.
6. Close the **Overhead/Profit by Cost Type** window. Do not update.
7. In the **Bid Items** window grid:
   
   1. Click the **Takeoff Details** tab.
   2. Next to the **Bid Items** cell, click the detail button.
   3. Select the **Overhead** and **Profit** columns, press F7, and set the security.
