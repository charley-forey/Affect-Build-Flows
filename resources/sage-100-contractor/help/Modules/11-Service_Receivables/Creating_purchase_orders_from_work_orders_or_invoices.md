### Creating purchase orders from work orders or invoices

Note: Service Receivables features are available only if you have purchased the [Service Receivables Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management).

In the **11-2 Work Orders/Invoices/Credits** window, you can create a purchase order for the material contained in a work order or invoice.

To create a purchase order from a work order or invoice, on the **Options** menu, click **Create Purchase Order**.
