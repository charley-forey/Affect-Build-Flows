### Refreshing the Dispatch Board

Note: Service Receivables features are available only if you have purchased the [Service Receivables Add-On Module](http://na.sage.com/sage-100-contractor/modules/service-management).

When you click **Refresh** on the menu bar, Sage 100 Contractor updates the **Dispatch Board** to reflect all recent changes made to appointments.

#### To refresh the Dispatch Board:

|  | 1 | Open **11-3 Dispatch Board**. |
|---|---|---|

|  | 2 | On the menu bar, select **Refresh**. |
