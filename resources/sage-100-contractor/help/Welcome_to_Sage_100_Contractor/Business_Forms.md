## Business forms

Sage 100 Contractor is designed to work with generic cheques and forms that are available from many sources. There is no single vendor that customers must use; however, you can get a complete portfolio of forms, tax forms, and cheques through Sage Construction and Real Estate Forms by calling 800-760-7929 or by visiting [www.sagecreforms.biz](http://www.sagecreforms.biz/).
