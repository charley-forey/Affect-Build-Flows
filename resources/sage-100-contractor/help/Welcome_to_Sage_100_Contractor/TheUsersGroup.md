# The Users Group

TUG, The Users Group for Sage 300 Construction and Real Estate, Sage 100 Contractor, and Sage Estimating is a not-for-profit, professional organization committed to supporting the users of Sage 100 Contractor and other Sage construction software.

You can find out more about the group, their services, and how to become a member at [http://tugweb.site-ym.com/default.asp?](http://tugweb.site-ym.com/default.asp?).
