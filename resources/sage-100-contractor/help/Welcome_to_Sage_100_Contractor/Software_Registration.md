# Software Registration

When registering the Sage 100 Contractor software, you need to enter the Sage Customer ID# and Registration Name provided to you by Sage.

#### Finding your Sage Company Name and Customer ID in the Customer Portal

1. Log into the Sage Customer Portal at [https://support.na.sage.com/](https://support.na.sage.com/).
2. Click on the [**Support**] tab.
3. Click on the [**Self Service**] link.
4. Click on the [**My Products**] tab.
5. Click on the [**Details Sheet**] link in the left frame.
6. Click the [**Show Advanced Search**] button.
7. Click the [**Go**] button.
8. Click on the **Customer Information** column header on the left.
9. Your Customer ID is in the **Customer** column, and your Sage Company Name is in the **Company Name** column.
